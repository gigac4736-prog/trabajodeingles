The origins of Pascua

History:
Pascua started in the year 33 A.D in Jerusalem at the time of Jesus death, exactly the third day.
Why we celebrate Pascua:
Pascua is celebrated because it remembers the resurrection of Jesus. It is the most important celebration for Christians.

Many movies and songs tell the story of Pascua. For example, in the film The Passion of the Christ, directed by Mel Gibson, the actor Jim Caviezel plays Jesus. His performance is perfect. The soundtrack and lyrics are perfects
Where Pascua is celebrated
Usually, Pascua is celebrated all around the world, mainly in the United States and Europe. The most popular way is with decorated chocolate eggs.
Curiosities about Pascua
The word "Pascua" comes from the Jewish holiday "Pesaj" that celebrates the freedom of Jews from Egypt.
